# Atlas

## Engineering Intelligence for Enterprise Capture

---

## Mission

Atlas exists to eliminate reactive service.

Customers should never discover scanner problems before Atlas does.

Atlas continuously observes, learns, predicts, and recommends actions across every capture device in the enterprise.

Eventually Atlas should resolve many issues automatically.

---

## Vision

Most fleet software tells customers what already happened.

Atlas tells customers what is about to happen.

Every deployment makes Atlas smarter.

Every scanner contributes knowledge.

Every repair improves future recommendations.

Every customer benefits from previous experience.

---

## Principles

### Observe

Discover every capture device automatically.

Understand:

- Hardware
- Firmware
- Drivers
- Software
- Operating system
- Environment

### Understand

Collect meaningful telemetry.

Never just raw data.

Transform data into knowledge.

Examples:

- Page counts
- Scan speed
- Consumables
- Maintenance
- Firmware
- Logs
- Event Viewer
- USB stability
- OCR timing
- Queue latency

### Learn

Every issue becomes part of Atlas.

Never lose engineering knowledge.

If Atlas solves a problem once...

It should help solve it forever.

### Predict

Atlas should answer:

> What will fail?

Before anyone asks:

> What failed?

### Recommend

Atlas never says:

> Something is wrong.

Atlas says:

> Replace the feed roller.
>
> Confidence: 96%
>
> Estimated repair: 11 minutes.

### Automate

Long term, Atlas should:

- Create tickets
- Notify customers
- Schedule technicians
- Recommend parts
- Order inventory
- Verify repairs

---

## Product Philosophy

No dashboard should exist simply to display data.

Every screen should answer a question.

Examples:

- What needs attention?
- Why?
- How confident are we?
- What should I do next?

---

## AI Philosophy

AI is never the product.

Engineering Intelligence is the product.

AI simply makes Engineering Intelligence possible.

---

## User Experience

The first screen should never overwhelm.

Atlas should greet users like this:

> Good morning.
>
> Here's what changed overnight.
>
> Here's what needs your attention.
>
> Here's what you can safely ignore.

---

## Success

Customers eventually say:

> We haven't had an unexpected scanner outage in months.

Instead of:

> We like your dashboard.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Atlas becomes the world's largest engineering knowledge graph for enterprise capture.

Every scanner teaches Atlas something.

Every customer benefits.

Every deployment improves everyone.

Every line of code should move Atlas closer to eliminating reactive service.
